JOURNEY 4 — Invoice payment confirmation (AR cash application)
Owner review pack  ·  try this in Architect; mark anything that does not match

What this proves
----------------
Same Nexus Architect as bank recon and 3-way match. Different finance process.
An AR analyst confirms which invoices were paid from a noisy bank statement.
No hardcoded cash-application template — the conversation + files design the pipeline.

This journey is also the fixture for rich Super Agent output: Dashboard charts,
How-it-works spreadsheet row justification, and on-demand Excel/PDF reports.

User: AR / collections analyst, manufacturing, O2C.
Goal: Match bank receipts to the invoice register by customer name, check amounts
against a 1% or USD 50 tolerance, flag unpaid / short / duplicate items, download Excel.

v1 note: download only (PDF/Excel). No Slack, email, ERP write-back, or tickets.

WARM-UP (3 general questions)

  1.  I'm an AR collections analyst on the O2C team.

  2.  Manufacturing — mid-market industrial, USD only.

  3.  Confirm which customer invoices actually got paid from the bank feed, and surface shorts and unpaid items.

USE CASE

  4.  Invoice payment confirmation. Match bank statement receipts to the customer invoice register by customer name, check whether the amount paid matches the invoice total, and tell me for each invoice if it is paid, short, overpaid, or not paid.

INPUT FILES

  5.  Yes — I have the June bank statement and the customer invoice register.

      [Attach]  Bank_Statement_June.xlsx
                Customer_Invoices.xlsx

KNOWLEDGE / POLICY

  6.  Yes — attaching our AR collections and cash application policy.

      [Attach]  AR_Collections_Policy.pdf

INTERVIEW (typical answers — wait for Nexus, then paste)

  7.  Pair on customer name. Bank lockbox names are messy — Northwind Wholesale Inc may show as NORTHWIND WHSLE CO. Do not use amount as a match key because shorts still need to pair.

  8.  After the name match, compare invoice total to the amount received. Allow the lesser of one percent of the invoice or fifty dollars. Ten dollars under is fine; thousands under is not.

  9.  One invoice can be paid with two deposits. Match them as a group. If cash is left over or a second full payment lands, call it out.

 10.  Anything unpaid more than thirty days past due is collections. Cite the AR policy so the reviewer knows why.

 11.  Excel workbook I can download — a sheet for paid, a sheet for partials, a sheet for exceptions. No Slack.

 12.  Read the point-by-point summary. If it is right, click Confirm pipeline.

      [Click] Run

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE IN THE REPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • INV-24-6001 Helios Stores NA  $128,440.15  — exact, paid
  • INV-24-6002 Northwind Wholesale Inc vs NORTHWIND WHSLE CO  $48,500.00  — name variant, paid
  • INV-24-6003 Acme Distributors  invoiced $45,200.00 / received $45,190.00  — $10 short, inside tolerance
  • INV-24-6004 Contoso Manufacturing  invoiced $88,400.00 / received $79,560.00  — out of tolerance, flagged
  • INV-24-6005 Fastenal  invoiced $12,000.00 / received $15,000.00  — overpay, flagged
  • INV-24-6006 Dealer AR Midwest  $32,000.00 in two deposits ($20k + $12k)  — split, paid
  • INV-24-6007 Old Dominion Freight  $22,100.00  — never paid, past due, escalate
  • INV-24-6008 ABM Industry  $6,400.00 paid twice  — duplicate, flagged
  • Payroll, utilities, fees, sweep stay unmatched operating noise — not customer issues

How it works: click INV-24-6003 and see
  abs(45190.00 - 45200.00) = 10.00  vs  min(0.01 * 45200.00, 50.00) = 50.00  → pass
Click INV-24-6004 and see
  abs(79560.00 - 88400.00) = 8840.00  vs  50.00  → fail
